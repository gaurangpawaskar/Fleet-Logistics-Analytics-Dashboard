# Fleet & Logistics Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Recommended Structure and Order

---

# 1. Project Title / Headline

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze fleet operations, logistics performance, shipment tracking, transportation efficiency, and supply chain KPIs through advanced business intelligence reporting.

---

# 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Fleet & Logistics Analytics Dashboard provides comprehensive insights into transportation and logistics operations by tracking shipment performance, delivery timelines, fleet utilization, transportation costs, and operational efficiency.

The dashboard helps logistics teams optimize delivery operations and improve supply chain visibility.

---

# 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 Power BI Desktop — Dashboard development and visualization
- 🔄 Power Query — Data cleaning and transformation
- 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) — KPI calculations and dynamic measures
- 📁 Excel / CSV Dataset — Logistics and shipment data source
- 📈 Interactive Charts & Filters — Used for drill-down and operational analysis

---

# 4. Data Source

The dashboard uses logistics and transportation datasets containing:

- Shipment details
- Fleet information
- Delivery timelines
- Transportation costs
- Route performance
- Vehicle utilization
- Order and inventory tracking

The dataset represents real-world logistics and supply chain operations.

---

# 5. Features / Highlights

## • Business Problem

Logistics companies handle large-scale shipment and transportation operations daily. Monitoring delivery efficiency, transportation costs, and fleet performance manually is difficult and inefficient.

Organizations require real-time operational insights to improve logistics planning and supply chain optimization.

---

## • Goal of the Dashboard

To provide a centralized analytics solution for monitoring fleet performance, shipment tracking, logistics KPIs, and transportation efficiency.

The dashboard enables operational teams to make faster and data-driven logistics decisions.

---

## • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

### 📌 KPI Cards
- Total Shipments
- On-Time Deliveries
- Delayed Deliveries
- Transportation Cost
- Fleet Utilization Rate

### 📌 Shipment Trend Analysis
Visualization of shipment volume and delivery performance over time.

### 📌 Delivery Performance
Analysis of delayed vs on-time deliveries across regions and routes.

### 📌 Fleet Utilization
Vehicle usage tracking and operational efficiency monitoring.

### 📌 Route Analysis
Comparison of transportation routes based on cost and delivery time.

### 📌 Regional Logistics Performance
Geographical analysis of shipment operations and regional logistics KPIs.

---

## • Business Impact & Insights

- Improves transportation efficiency
- Helps reduce delivery delays
- Enables better fleet management
- Supports logistics cost optimization
- Enhances shipment visibility
- Improves supply chain decision-making
